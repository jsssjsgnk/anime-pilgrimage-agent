# Tokyo Metro barrier-free facilities

Tokyo Metro defines a barrier-free “one-route” as at least one route connecting street level, ticket gate, and platform using an elevator, slope, stairlift, or wheelchair-accessible escalator.

Station pages list elevators inside and outside gates, accessible toilets, and route-specific details. Equipment can be unavailable during inspection or construction, so a low-walking traveler should check the station's current facility notice shortly before travel.
