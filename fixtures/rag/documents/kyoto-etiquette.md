# Respectful photography in Kyoto

Kyoto visitor guidance asks travelers to check and obey no-photography notices. Photography may be limited to designated areas in parts of Gion, shrines, temples, restaurants, and residential streets.

Treat private lanes and homes as residents' space. Do not block narrow roads, follow or stop local people for photographs, or enter private property. When a location-specific notice differs from general advice, the posted local rule should be reconfirmed on the day of the visit.
